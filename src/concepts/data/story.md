# The Origin

In October 2024 we launched our first event, bringing in Bitcoin and freedom tech enthusiasts to ask, "What should we do?". The answer was overwhelmingly education to help attendees level up their skills. While there are events of all stripes every day in New York City, a gap in the market was hands-on classes, workshops, and demonstrations teaching practical skills and tools in the latest and greatest in freedom tech.

We quickly learned to take a two-pronged educational approach, broadening our reach with educational material accessible to the layperson while fostering an environment for our most advanced and technically proficient members to collaborate and teach others.

# How We've Grown

Keeping Bitcoin and the cypherpunk ethos at the core, we've since expanded focus to topics such as sovereign AI, open source tools, self-hosting, and more. We now have a robust community of highly technically proficient professionals that stay on the cutting edge of digital privacy, sovereignty, and freedom.

With the launch of our own physical space in the heart of Manhattan, we're laying a strong foundation to deepen our community roots and engagement, increase event frequency, and expand our reach in New York City.

# Where We're Headed

Our next two years will be focused on three core tenants:

1. Community
2. Growth
3. Bringing truly useful + usable freedom tech to NYC

With our focus in these three areas, we'll broaden our membership, deepen our pool of technically capable users, and act as a guiding hand to get them to develop and spur the adopt ion of freedom tech tools in New York City of genuine value to its users.

After building our profile, reach, and impact both locally and online, we'll rent our own ground-floor building in Manhattan, using clear and distinct and clear branding stand out as the clear hub for authentic freedom tech enthusiasts in the area. Given New York City's relevance globally, this presents the opportunity to be one of the world's most prominent and impactful freedom tech hubs in the world.
